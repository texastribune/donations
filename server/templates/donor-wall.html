Our Supporters

Individuals & Foundations Corporate Sponsors

Individuals who share The Texas Tribune’s belief that promoting greater civic engagement and informed discourse is a direct route to a better and more productive Texas support this organization with tax-deductible donations. A donation of any amount makes you a Texas Tribune member for one year. Tribune members play no role in guiding the journalism produced by the Tribune or the planning and execution of events.

You can make a gift online to become a Texas Tribune member today!

The Tribune is committed to full transparency into our funding. Below you can see total donations from the last 365 days and gifts pledged through the end of this year. Amounts listed are updated daily and are subject to change. Donation amounts from 2009 through October 14, 2025 can be viewed here. If you have a question about an amount, please email membership@texastribune.org.

When Texas Tribune reporting includes individuals or corporations who have given $1,000 or more to this organization, we disclose their names at the bottom of the article.

The Texas Tribune also earns income from Texas Tribune Festival ticket sales; outside rentals of Studio 919, our events space; and subscriptions to The Blast, our politics newsletter. That revenue is not reported here but can be found in aggregate on our most recent Form 990 found on ourfinancial documents page.
